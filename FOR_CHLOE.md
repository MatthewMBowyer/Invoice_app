# FOR CHLOE — your invoice app, now on your phone from anywhere

This is your tutoring invoice app. It works in a web browser, so you can use it
from your phone, your laptop, or any computer — the same app, the same invoices.
You do not install anything and there is nothing to update.

Your clients, lessons and invoices are kept in **your own Google Drive**. Not on
someone else's computer, and not somewhere anyone else can see. When you sign in
with your Google account, the app reads and writes only your own file, in your
own Drive.

---

## 1. Opening the app

1. On your phone, open the web browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. Type this address, or tap the link if it has been sent to you:

   **https://matthewmbowyer.github.io/Invoice_app/**

3. You will see the purple-teal logo and a button that says **Sign in with
   Google**.

Tip: once it is open, use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** (the Share
button on iPhone → *Add to Home Screen*). It then opens like a normal app icon,
and it is much easier to find next time.

---

## 2. Signing in

Tap **Sign in with Google** and pick your Google account. That is the only sign
in there is.

The first time you do this, Google asks whether you want to let the app see a
file in your Drive. Choose **Allow**. The app only ever sees the one file it
makes for you — it cannot see your photos, your email attachments, or any other
document in your Drive.

You only have to do this once per device. After that it signs you in quietly.

---

## 3. Where your information is kept

Your information lives in a single Google file called a **spreadsheet**, with a
tab for each kind of thing:

- **Settings** — your business name, banking details, rates and wording
- **Clients** — the parents you invoice
- **Learners** — the children you tutor
- **Lessons**
- **Invoices** — every invoice you have made

It is a normal Google Sheet. If you are curious you can open Google Drive on your
computer, find the file the app made, and read it. Do not edit it by hand — the
app is the thing that keeps it tidy — but it is yours, and it is readable.

**Backing up.** In the app, go to **Backup** and tap **Export JSON backup**. Keep
that file in your Drive next to the spreadsheet. If anything ever goes wrong with
the sheet, that file is a full copy you can restore from with **Restore from a
JSON backup** on the same screen.

---

## 4. Making an invoice

From the home screen, tap the big **Create Invoice** button at the top. There are
only three steps.

1. **Who is it for?** Type the parent's name. Pick one of your saved parents from
   the list, or type a brand-new name — the app saves them for you, so you never
   have to go and add them first. If the name is new it asks for their mobile
   number, because it needs one to send the invoice later. The child box is
   optional.
2. **Rate and hours.** Pick a rate, or type a different price. The total appears
   as you go. If you are charging one price rather than an hourly rate, tick
   **Charge one total instead of hours** and type the amount — it shows on the
   invoice as *flat*. You can add another line for a second rate, and every line
   has its own **Remove line** button named after it.
3. **Dates.** The number is filled in for you, the date is today, and the due
   date is worked out for you. Change the dates if you need to. Leave **Remember
   this rate for this parent** ticked and the app fills the price in next time.

Tap **Save invoice**. The invoice is saved into your Drive and you are shown your
invoice list.

---

## 5. Sending it on WhatsApp

Open **Invoices**, tap the invoice, then tap **Open WhatsApp**. Your message is
already written and the parent's number is already in it. Send it there.

The app cannot send it for you and cannot tell whether it arrived — WhatsApp
keeps that to itself. You will also want to attach the PDF: tap **Download PDF**
first, then in WhatsApp use the paperclip to attach the file you just saved.

---

## 6. If signing in runs out

Google sign-ins last about an hour, and then quietly renew in the background
while you keep using the app.

Sometimes Google will ask you to sign in again rather than renew quietly. When
that happens you will see, in plain words:

> **Your Google sign-in expired. Tap to sign in again.**

Tap the button and pick your account again. Nothing is lost — your invoices are
already safely in your Drive.

---

## 7. Signing out

Tap **Sign out** at the top right. The app forgets your sign-in straight away and
shows the sign-in screen again. To see your invoices again, sign in again.

---

## 8. If something looks wrong

- **The page looks empty.** Pull down to refresh, then sign in again.
- **"Could not save."** The invoice was **not** saved. Your typing is still on
  the screen. Check your internet connection and tap **Save invoice** again.
- **A number you did not expect.** The app never reuses or skips an invoice
  number, even for a cancelled invoice. If you cancel an invoice, its number is
  used up on purpose.

Anything confusing, or anything that does not match how you actually invoice —
say so. That is the most useful feedback there is, and it gets fixed.
